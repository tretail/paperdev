# Product context

Copy this file to `docs/product-context.md` in a product's repository. The
Product Lead reads it before every discovery run and treats it as your word.
Short and concrete beats complete; update it when you learn something.

## Vision
One or two sentences: what this product makes possible, and for whom.

## Users and jobs-to-be-done
- **Primary user:** who they are, their situation, what they're trying to get done.
- **Secondary users:** e.g. staff, guests, admins.

## Core loop
How a user gets value, comes back, and brings others in.

## Positioning and competitors
- Main alternatives users have today (products or workarounds).
- Why we win / where we're weaker.

## Current focus
This quarter's product goals and what is explicitly *not* a priority.

## Signals
Paste customer feedback, support themes, sales objections, and key metrics
(activation, retention, conversion). Dates help.

## Constraints
Brand and tone, platforms (web, mobile, tablet in a kitchen…), regulation,
pricing model.
